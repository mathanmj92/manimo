# Design: Manimo landing page

Surface: `site/index.html` (Persuade). World: **a Manim scene on a chalkboard**. The page uses Manim's own colours on the app's Chalkboard (dark) and Paper (light) looks, so the site and the app read as one product. The hero is a real Manim classic, a sine wave growing out of a circle, drawn live in SVG. Product truth lives in `/PRODUCT.md`.

## Type

- Display: Bricolage Grotesque (variable, self-hosted), weight 700 to 800, tracking -0.02 to -0.035em, `text-wrap: balance`. H1 max 6rem.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk 400 to 700, 17px, line height 1.65, measure 62ch.
- Code: JetBrains Mono, only for real code, file names and the folder tree.

## Shapes and motion

- A faint number-plane grid (like Manim's NumberPlane) sits behind the hero, the lesson and the voices sections.
- One authored motion: the hero circle draws, then a point travels round it while its height is traced out as a sine wave. It has Pause and Replay buttons, and reduced motion shows the finished figure. The step line draws once when scrolled into view.
- Cards are avoided. Voices are a list with players, steps are a line, tools are a chip strip, About is a definition list.

## Components

Accent pill button (on-accent text), line pill button, round theme toggle (sun/moon), voice row with play button and meter, framed screenshot (`.shot`), FAQ disclosure list, chip strip (`.tools`), link chips (`.linkrow`), definition list (`.facts`).

## Provenance of images and media

- `assets/img/*.webp`: real screenshots of the running Manimo app (version 1.9), captured at 2x, showing the "Physics lesson" demo project. `hover-help` has its top 84px cropped to remove a stray tooltip. The assistant screenshot shows an example conversation whose steps and numbers come from a real run (bubble sort, 50 animations, clean in 1 round); it is labelled as an example on the page.
- `assets/capacitor-lesson.mp4`, `capacitor-poster.webp`, `capacitor-lesson.vtt`: a lesson rendered by Manimo, narrated by the built-in Adam voice; captions written by ManimGX at render time.
- `assets/audio/*.m4a`: samples generated by the four built-in voices.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque, Hanken Grotesk and JetBrains Mono, SIL Open Font Licence, from Google Fonts.
- Prompt builder and library: `assets/prompts.json` is the source (master template, fields, library, follow-ups); `js/prompts.js` is generated from it. Rules in the template come from `skills/manimo-animator`. The library entries' numbers (RC tau = 1 s, projectile range about 40.8 m, SHM T about 3.14 s) were checked by hand.
- Page sections were trimmed to: hero, lesson video, app, prompt, library, write, render, voices, compose, why, FAQ, contact, download. About, steps, projects and looks were removed on request. Contact uses mathannits92@gmail.com.
