# Surface brief: Manimo landing page (site/index.html)

Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: teachers, students and tutors (many in India) who want narrated maths and science videos without learning Python. Action: download Manimo for Windows (free). Proof on hand: the real app (sharp screenshots of every feature), one real narrated lesson video (51 s capacitor charging), four real voice samples. Constraints: no invented testimonials, user counts, benchmarks or prices; every claim is something the app does today; works at phone width, keyboard only, reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A kolam is one line drawn around a grid of dots, and Manimo makes the same thing for a lesson: a line that draws itself, step by step, until the idea is clear. The page refuses the SaaS hero-plus-three-cards arrangement and the chalkboard/3Blue1Brown-navy look every maths tool wears.

OWN-WORLD: Wet black earth ground (#17110e), rice-flour white linework and text (#f3efe6), marigold (#f4a300) as the one live colour, kavi red-brown (#b6422d) used only for border bands, banana-leaf green for "done" states. Lines are monoline and rounded like flour poured by hand; dots (pulli) are the recurring unit: bullets, step markers, dividers, the grid behind diagrams. A light band in rice-flour white shows the Paper look. Display type is a variable grotesque with optical size; code in a clean mono.

STORY: A visitor understands within seconds that this turns code into a finished, narrated lesson video on their own PC; believes it because every feature is shown in the real app; acts by pressing Download for Windows (free), repeated after the proof and at the close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the headline "Math, in motion." at very large scale, a one-sentence promise, the Download for Windows button (marigold) and a quiet "See a lesson made" link. Right, a large kolam (a dotted diamond lattice of four-lobed loops) draws itself line by line on load, with a "Draw another" control that redraws it at three sizes. The page's one memorable thing is that line.

FORM: Position 7 of my ordered list of seven (chalkboard, 3Blue1Brown-style navy, lab graph paper, blueprint, overhead acetate, two-colour textbook plate, kolam); seed key bc2d34d4.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
